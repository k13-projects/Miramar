Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 20, 2026

Miramar Food Hall (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the website miramarfoodhall.com (the “Site”). This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We provide this policy to comply with the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and other applicable privacy laws.

Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to provide it to us — for example, when you email us or submit a contact or event-inquiry form. This may include:

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and (if you provide it) phone number.
  • Message content — any information you include in your message or inquiry, such as event date, guest count, or other details you share.

We also automatically collect limited, aggregated, anonymized usage data through privacy-friendly analytics (see “Analytics” below). This data does not identify you personally.

How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiries, messages, and event or vendor requests;
  • Communicate with you about your request; and
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Categories of Third Parties

We may share personal information only with service providers who help us operate the Site and respond to you, and only as needed for those purposes:

  • Hosting and analytics provider (Vercel) — hosts the Site and provides aggregated, cookieless traffic analytics.
  • Email provider — delivers messages you send to us.
  • Legal compliance — where required by law, legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand aggregate site traffic and performance. These tools are cookieless and collect only anonymized, aggregated data; they do not track you across other websites and do not build an individual profile of you.

Cookies and “Do Not Track”

The Site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not track visitors across third-party sites over time. Because we do not engage in such tracking, we do not respond differently to browser “Do Not Track” (DNT) signals. Some browsers and devices offer privacy controls you can use at any time.

How to Review or Change Your Information

You may request to review, update, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided to us by emailing info@miramarfoodhall.com. We will respond to verifiable requests within a reasonable time.

Children’s Privacy

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will revise the “Effective date” above and post the updated policy on this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.
