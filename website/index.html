Our Story

OUR STORY

1938

Miramar Food Hall was born from a vision to bring new life to one of San Clemente's most treasured landmarks. Once home to the Miramar Theatre and the San Clemente Bowling Center, this 1938 Spanish Colonial Revival gem had been closed for over three decades.

2020

In 2020, we took on the challenge of restoring the space, honoring its rich history while transforming it into a vibrant culinary destination. Today, Miramar Food Hall stands as a celebration of community, global bites, local vibes and the enduring spirit of Southern California, marking the long-awaited revival of an iconic coastal landmark.

2026

Rooted in the relaxed spirit of coastal California, Miramar Food Hall brings together the artistry of local chefs and global flavors under one roof. Designed as a family-friendly gathering place, we offer an elevated yet approachable dining experience that reflects a balance of tradition and innovation, celebrating the region's diverse culinary scene while honoring the charm and character of this historic setting.

MEET OUR VENDORS

Cosmos Burger

Burgers made with fresh, premium ingredients with locations in Oceanside and Carlsbad, CA. Best known for the signature Spicy Jam Burger and Monkey Fries.

El Puerto Street Tacos

Authentic Mexican street tacos, burritos, and quesadillas made with fresh ingredients since 2005, with two Carlsbad, California locations.

Graciously Thai

Late night destination for authentic, home-style Thai food with a location in Temecula, CA.

Hen Haus

A brand new woman and family-owned concept from Thyda Sieng of Long Beach's acclaimed Fire Bird (FB) Nashville Hot Chicken, which shuttered in 2021.

Immersion Coffee Co.

Community-focused coffee shop, formerly Manivela Coffee Truck, with locations in San Diego's Little Italy and La Jolla neighborhoods.

It's Allll Rice

Fried Rice with no Rules. First brick-and-mortar location for the globally-inspired fried rice-focused concept.

La Vida

Healthy eats including salads, sandwiches and wraps, toasts, and smoothies with a location in Carlsbad, CA.

Lobster Lab

Seafood concept with a location in Carlsbad, CA serving up various lobster roll iterations, including the popular Lobster Grilled Cheese. Number 1 Ranked Lobster Roll in San Diego 2024 on Yelp.

MOTO Pizza

The first out-of-state outpost for the acclaimed Seattle-based pizza chain known for its "odd pizza," combining Detroit, New York, and Roman styles with Filipino influences.

Norigiri

Fresh, modern take on Japanese onigiri - handmade rice balls with bold fillings - served fast and packed with flavor, with a location in Irvine.

RolledUp SC

Sushi rolls and customizable burritos and bowls, with a location in Carlsbad, CA.

Sidelines Sandwiches

The concept started as a pop-up at Del Mar, CA's Surf Sports Park (formerly the San Diego Polo Fields), serving sandwiches on the "sidelines" of youth soccer tournaments.

The Pita

A Middle Eastern concept, serving shawarma, gyros, pita, hummus, and more, from the husband and wife owners of San Clemente's Rocket Fizz soda pop & candy shop.
