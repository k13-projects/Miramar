What Actually Applies

The Rules Governing This Site

A private food-hall marketing site is not subject to Section 508 (that binds federal agencies). The real framework is the ADA, the WCAG accessibility guidelines courts rely on, and California’s privacy and civil-rights statutes.

RequirementApplies?Where We Stand
Section 508 (Rehabilitation Act)Not ApplicableBinds federal agencies & fund recipients — not a private restaurant.
ADA Title III — physical venueMandatedRestaurants are “public accommodations.” Premises accessibility is assessed separately from the website.
ADA Title III — websiteReachableVia the 9th-Circuit “nexus” rule (Robles v. Domino’s). No fixed technical standard in law, so WCAG is the benchmark.
WCAG 2.1 Level AAConformsThe de-facto standard. Automated + manual checks pass; accessibility score 100/100.
Unruh Civil Rights Act (CA)MitigatedTurns any ADA gap into $4,000+/violation. Risk lowered by removing the issues scanners flag.
CalOPPA privacy policyPublishedRequired because forms collect personal info. Policy now live & linked in the footer.
CCPA / CPRANot TriggeredRevenue / data-volume thresholds not met by a single-location food hall.
Cookie consent bannerNot RequiredAnalytics are cookieless & anonymized; no GDPR-style banner needed in the US.
CAN-SPAMIf Email MarketingOnly triggers if a newsletter/promotional email program is launched.
Work Completed

What We Put In Place

  • ✓
    WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility — darkened brand teal to pass color-contrast minimums, added a keyboard-state to the menu, “reduce motion” support, and corrected heading order. Accessibility score: 100.
  • ✓
    Privacy Policy page (CalOPPA) — covers data collected, third parties, analytics, Do-Not-Track, and user rights. Linked in every footer.
  • ✓
    Accessibility Statement page — declares the WCAG 2.1 AA target and a contact route for issues — documented good-faith effort.
  • ✓
    No accessibility “overlay” widgets — deliberately avoided; they don’t confer compliance and the FTC fined a major overlay vendor $1M. Accessibility is handled in code.
  • ✓
    Placeholder phone number removed — a fictional number had been live since launch; pulled until the real one is confirmed.
  • ✓
    Performance & delivery — images converted to WebP with safe fallbacks, oversized assets resized, third-party feed lazy-loaded. Performance 71 → 92.
