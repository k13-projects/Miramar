## Minor, non-blocking note (flagging for design/eng, not from this diff)

`.open-now__inner` carries a pre-existing `-webkit-box-reflect` (added 2026-06-24, commit `5de5aac`, the "We Are Open Now" restyle) that reflects the badge text downward with a fading gradient. Against the old flat teal gradient background this reflection was nearly invisible; against the new, busier hero video it now reads as a faint ghost/double-exposure text artifact overlapping the top of the headline on some frames (see crop evidence below). It does not block legibility of the actual `h1` text, so it's not a fail against the two items asked — but it's a visual regression the video surfaced, worth a follow-up pass (either drop the box-reflect or nudge the overlay opacity up slightly).
